## How these names are used

These names are used nominatively. That means they are used only as
reasonably necessary to identify the third-party device, software, or
content that QC Remote interoperates with, or to report a name the device
itself shows. QC Remote uses no more of any mark than identification
requires. The repository contains no Neural DSP logo, and nothing in it is
intended to suggest sponsorship or endorsement.

Some protocol identifiers carry Neural DSP product names, for example the
`cortex_control_version` field and the `CORTEX_CONTROL_VERSION` constant
(`packages/rust/quadcortex/src/profile.rs`,
`packages/rust/quadcortex/src/device/initialization.rs`,
`apps/android/android/app/src/main/java/org/freevia/qcremote/QcUsbProfile.java`).
These name protocol fields the device defines. They are reproduced for
interoperability. QC Remote is not Cortex Control and does not claim to be.

No trademark license, certification, partnership, or guarantee of
compatibility is claimed. Device compatibility is firmware-sensitive. QC
Remote does not contain, replace, or modify Quad Cortex® firmware.

QC Remote's control surface draws its own reconstructions of CorOS screens,

Those reconstructions are written for interoperability. They are not Neural
DSP assets, they are not supplied or approved by Neural DSP, and they may
