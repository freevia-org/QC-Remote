# Code of conduct

QC Remote adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct for issues, pull requests, discussions, and every other
project space.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep criticism about the work, and
do not harass anyone. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads,
and block participants who do not follow it.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately by email to **security@freevia.org**, with
"Code of conduct" in the subject line so they are not mistaken for
vulnerability reports. Do not report them in a public issue. Reports are
handled by the maintainers at Freevia EOOD, the project's publisher, and are kept
confidential as far as the situation allows.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines.
